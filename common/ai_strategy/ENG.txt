# HER British / Commonwealth AI strategy
#
# Design target:
# - RAF and home defence before prestige land commitments;
# - protect Atlantic and imperial convoy routes against submarine warfare;
# - preserve a limited expeditionary army in 1939-40 instead of dying in Benelux/France;
# - hold Suez, Gibraltar, Malta and the route to India;
# - support Burma/Singapore when Japan enters the war;
# - shift toward offensive amphibious warfare only after the empire is secure.
