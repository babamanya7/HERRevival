# HER French AI strategy
#
# Design target:
# - defend metropolitan France without draining the army into colonies or Norway;
# - hold the Maginot line economically while keeping mobile reserves for the north;
# - maintain a serious fighter force and artillery-heavy army;
# - use armor as a mobile reserve / counterattack force rather than a tiny token arm;
# - avoid obsolete vanilla naval-role production controls; VNR ship production lives in naval_production.txt.
