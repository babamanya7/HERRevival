# HER German AI strategy
#
# Design target:
# - rapid but controlled rearmament before 1939;
# - concentrated armored warfare in Poland and the West;
# - strong fighter/CAS arm and reliable logistics;
# - historically dominant U-boat effort rather than prestige surface expansion;
# - deliberate Barbarossa preparation, highly aggressive opening, then a more sustainable Eastern war;
# - react to Allied landings instead of feeding every reserve into the Soviet front.
