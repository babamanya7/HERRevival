# HER Italian AI strategy
#
# Design target:
# - finish Ethiopia efficiently without overcommitting after the European war starts;
# - build a mostly infantry/artillery army with a limited mobile reserve;
# - maintain a credible Regia Aeronautica and active Mediterranean fleet;
# - prioritize Libya, Sicily, Sardinia and the central Mediterranean instead of wasting divisions across Axis fronts;
# - enter the major war on a historical timetable, then fight for Egypt/Suez and defend the peninsula.
