# HER Japanese AI strategy
#
# Design target:
# - China is the primary land war through the late 1930s;
# - limited armor, strong infantry/artillery and useful specialist marines;
# - carrier aviation and screens are the core of the Pacific fleet;
# - Southern Operation is fast and aggressive against PHI/MAL/INS;
# - the IJN remains operationally active after the opening invasions;
# - Japan avoids wasting the army in Australia/India too early;
# - from 1943 onward defend the outer perimeter and home waters while still contesting the Pacific.
