# HER Soviet AI strategy
#
# Design target:
# - industrial buildup before the European war;
# - strong but not all-consuming military ramp from 1939/40;
# - defensive operational behavior during the opening of Barbarossa;
# - limited winter counteroffensives rather than constant suicidal attacks;
# - increasingly offensive behavior from 1943 onward;
# - minimal distraction in the Far East while Germany is the primary threat.
